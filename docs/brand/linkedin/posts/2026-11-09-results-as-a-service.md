Hourly billing rewards effort. Per-user SaaS rewards headcount.

Neither rewards results.

So we sell Results as a Service:

1️⃣ We agree the result first, in writing, including how we'll both know it works
2️⃣ You pay one fixed price for that result
3️⃣ If we miss it, we fix it at no extra cost
4️⃣ After go-live, a monthly managed plan keeps it running
5️⃣ Any change is quoted at a fixed price before work starts

Our Kaizen PPM product works the same way: deployed once into your own Microsoft tenant, no per-user fee from us, then a managed plan.

And when the problem is still fuzzy, we don't force a fixed price. We start with a small, fixed-price discovery step instead.
