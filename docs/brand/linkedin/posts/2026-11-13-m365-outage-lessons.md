Yesterday a lot of teams found out how much of their day runs through Outlook and Teams.

Microsoft 365 had a global outage on Thursday. Outlook, Teams and Copilot were affected for many users. Microsoft says it wasn't a cyberattack and is still working on the root cause.

Outages happen to every cloud provider. What matters is what you do the day after. Five things worth checking today:

1️⃣ Know where Microsoft reports incidents. Admins: Microsoft 365 admin center → Health → Service health. Everyone else: status.cloud.microsoft.

2️⃣ Have one channel outside Microsoft 365. If email and Teams go down together, how does your team reach each other and your key customers?

3️⃣ Resubmit failed Power Automate runs. Approvals, notifications and syncs can quietly go missing.

4️⃣ Check agents and integrations that missed events. Look for gaps, not just errors.

5️⃣ List what stopped. Those processes need a fallback.
